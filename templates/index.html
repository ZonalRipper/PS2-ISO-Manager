Auto Pilot — End-to-End OPL Library Builder

Turn a folder of raw PS2 downloads or disc rips into a fully organized, named, compressed, and artwork-ready Open PS2 Loader (OPL) drive in one click.

⚡ What Auto Pilot Does Automatically (5-Stage Pipeline) Supports .ISO • .ZSO • .BIN/.CUE • .CHD
STAGE 1 💿

Convert Disc Images

Converts .BIN/.CUE (auto-recreates missing .CUE files) and .CHD dumps into PS2 .ISO images.

STAGE 2 🏷️

ID Scan & Clean Title

Reads SYSTEM.CNF inside the disc to find the Sony serial (SLUS/SLES) & official game title.

STAGE 3 🗜️

ZSO Compress / FAT32

Shrinks ISOs into OPL .ZSO files (saves ~25–40% space) or splits >4GB ISOs into ul.cfg chunks.

STAGE 4 📂

Smart OPL Routing

Builds OPL folders (DVD/, CD/, ART/, CFG/) & moves <700MB to CD and ≥700MB to DVD.

STAGE 5 🖼️

6-Piece Art & CFG

Downloads Front/Back covers, Spine, Disc icon, Logo & Background to ART/ + game info to CFG/.

⚡ PS2 ISO Manager

Version 1.0.0 (Open Source Web Edition)

A high-performance desktop utility designed to manage, convert, compress, rename, and organize PlayStation 2 game libraries for Open PS2 Loader (OPL).

Program Attributions & Credits

  • Open PS2 Loader (OPL): The community standard software loader platform for the PlayStation 2.
  • Luden02 OPL Art Database: Structured static Game ID artwork repositories used for automated cover, disc, and background scraping.
  • xlenore ps2-covers: Foundational cover art mapping schemas and community layouts.
  • ZSO Compression Tool (ziso): Fast, efficient OPL-compatible ISO compression format and binaries.
  • bchunk utility: Reliable BIN/CUE track conversion mapping for multi-track optical disc rips.
  • Three.js & OrbitControls: Powers the hardware-accelerated 3D WebGL game case and rotating optical disc preview system.
