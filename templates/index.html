Step 5 of 7 • Compression & Filesystem Est. savings calculated on scan

Compress games to .ZSO format?

Modern Open PS2 Loader builds support LZ4-compressed .ZSO disc images—typically saving 25%–40% drive space with no gameplay lag.

💽 APA / PFS Internal PS2 HDD Mode Active

Internal PS2 APA hard drives install games as raw HDLoader/OPL partitions via hdl_dump (inject_dvd / inject_cd), which require uncompressed .ISO data and have no 4 GB file limit.

  • .ZSO compression & FAT32 splitting are automatically bypassed.
  • Any existing .ZSO files in your Source folder will be automatically decompressed to a temporary .ISO before injection.
🗜️ Recommended

Yes — Compress to .ZSO

Shrinks .ISO images into OPL-ready .ZSO files to fit far more games on your drive.

💿 Uncompressed

No — Keep Standard .ISO

Skips compression and transfers standard uncompressed .ISO files for legacy OPL versions.

💾 Auto-split ISOs larger than 4 GB on FAT32 drives (ul.cfg)?

FAT32 Safety Net
Yes — Auto-Split >4GB on FAT32 Recommended

Automatically creates 1 GB USBUtil chunks + ul.cfg when destination is FAT32.

No — Never Split ISOs Disabled

Always copy whole files directly into DVD/ (requires exFAT, NTFS, or SMB).

⚡ PS2 ISO Manager

Version 1.5 (Open Source Desktop Edition)

A high-performance desktop utility designed to manage, convert, compress, rename, and organize PlayStation 2 game libraries for Open PS2 Loader (OPL) across USB, exFAT/FAT32, SMB, and internal APA / PFS PlayStation 2 hard drives.

Program Attributions & Credits

  • Open PS2 Loader (OPL): The community standard software loader platform for the PlayStation 2.
  • hdl_dump (ps2homebrew — The W1zard 0f 0z & AKuHAK): Open-source APA/HDL partition game installer, TOC reader, and title manager for internal PS2 hard drives.
  • pfsshell (ps2homebrew — uyjulian, Wizard of Oz & PS2SDK): PlayStation File System (PFS) shell and ps2sdk APA/PFS/iomanX libraries powering +OPL drive browsing, artwork/CFG uploads, and partition management.
  • Luden02 OPL Art Database: Structured static Game ID artwork repositories used for automated cover, disc, and background scraping.
  • xlenore ps2-covers: Foundational cover art mapping schemas and community layouts.
  • ZSO Compression Tool (ziso): Fast, efficient OPL-compatible LZ4 ISO compression and decompression utility.
  • bchunk & MAME chdman: Reliable BIN/CUE track conversion and Compressed Hunks of Data (.CHD) disc image extraction tools.
  • Three.js & OrbitControls: Powers the hardware-accelerated 3D WebGL game case and rotating optical disc preview system.
