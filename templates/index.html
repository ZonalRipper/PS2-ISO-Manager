Step 5 of 7 • Storage Format Saves ~25–40%

Compress ISOs to .ZSO?

Modern OPL supports LZ4-compressed .zso images—saving 25–40% drive space with zero gameplay lag.

💽 Internal PS2 HDD (APA / PFS) Mode

APA HDL partitions require raw uncompressed .ISO images and have no 4 GB file size limit. .ZSO compression and FAT32 splitting are automatically bypassed, and any .ZSO inputs in your Source folder will be auto-decompressed before injection.
